BEATLAB
Make beats, write them as code, build your own soundtrack.

Missions

    Studio

    16 steps = 1 bar. Each shaded group of 4 is one beat.

    Mood

    Beat Code

    Your grid, written as code. Change it and press Run.

    Shortcut: Ctrl + Enter

    Tip: every line is one instrument. Each character is one step.

    x
    hit the drum on this step
    .
    rest (silence)
    1-6
    a note, low (1) to high (6)
    |
    divider between beats, just for reading
    tempo 120
    speed, from 60 to 180
    mood race
    hero, happy, chill, spooky or race
    sound bell
    bleep, flute, buzz or bell
    # note
    a comment the computer ignores

    My Soundtrack

    A song is beats played in order.

    The name and the mood are the secret recipe. The same name and mood always make the same song, so try your pet's name, your street, or your best friend.
